Faith & Philosophy

My faith is at the core of everything I do. It forms my worldview, the way I look at the world and react to it. Here is a summary of my core beliefs.

Topic Belief Supporting Verses
God There is one God in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Matthew 28:19,
2 Corinthians 13:14
Jesus Christ Jesus is the Son of God, fully divine and fully human, who died for our sins and rose again. John 1:1,
John 1:14,
1 Corinthians 15:3-4
Salvation Salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone. Ephesians 2:8-9,
John 3:16
Scripture The Bible is the inspired, inerrant, and authoritative Word of God. 2 Timothy 3:16,
2 Peter 1:20-21
Church The Church is the body of Christ, consisting of all believers. 1 Corinthians 12:12-13,
Ephesians 4:4-6
