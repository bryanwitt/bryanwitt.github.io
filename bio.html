My Story

Hi there!

My name is Bryan Witt and I am a follower of Christ. That is my identity, how I want to be known. Of course, there’s more to me than that, but that my main goal is to follow Christ. You can learn more about that on the faith page.
My story started over 20 years ago in Kansas City, MO. At age 5 my family moved to Cincinnati, OH, where I have lived ever since.

Interests

As I’ve grown up I’ve developed many interests, most notably computers, music, and books.

Computers

I’ve enjoyed tinkering around with varying programming languages, including Python, JavaScript, and HTML/CSS. Among the projecs I've created is this website. I’ve also enjoyed tinkering with my Raspberry Pi.

Music

I enjoy listening to and playing music. I enjoy listening to a variety of music, including classical and gospel. I am proficient in piano, organ, and percussion, and especially enjoy improvising on the piano.

Books

One of my favorite things to do is to sit in a comfortable chair and read a book. In the busyness and hurry of life, I find that books can be a welcome escape. I enjoy just about any type of book, though my preference is for science, science fiction, and fantasy, and against romance. You can check out my book list here.

Character

If you asked one of my friends, they would tell you that I am impeccably honest. While I do have a sense of humor and may make a lot of jokes, I do my absolute best to be above reproach. I am a man of my word. I am very careful when handling money and confidential information and always make sure they end up in the right hands.
